How full drainage, drainage between reservoirs and stored water differ

The basin request used here includes the full upstream network. It does not stop at the next reservoir. It also includes the whole catchment containing the indexed flowline; a split at the exact gauge is a separate service option. A difference in area is a prompt to check the outlet and the area definition.

Deer Creek example, from the committed reservoir trace: the full basin above Jordanelle is about 252 sq mi. The full basin above Deer Creek is about 547 sq mi and includes the Jordanelle basin. If those boundaries are nested, subtracting the upstream basin leaves about 295 sq mi between the two dams. That difference is illustrative; no separate between-dam polygon has been verified here.

Do not add 252 and 547 to measure their combined land area: the upstream land overlaps. The land between the dams is about 54% of the full Deer Creek basin, not 54% of its flow or storage. Imports, diversions and operations need separate evidence.

The dashboard sums the actual stored acre-feet of distinct reservoirs in the selected set.
